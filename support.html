If you need to talk to someone

This inbox is mail, not a hotline. If tonight is heavy and you want a person on the line, the 988 Lifeline is free and open 24 hours, in the US. You can call or text, and you don’t have to be sure it’s a crisis to use it.

Call 988 | Text 988

If you’re looking for a counselor, the SAMHSA National Helpline is 1-800-662-4357. Free, 24 hours, English and Spanish, and its whole job is helping people find one near them.
